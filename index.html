Probability methodology & coverage

Player odds use official Hobby odds and equal-likelihood checklist assumptions where Topps does not publish card-level weighting. “Any modeled hit” combines base parallels, inserts and autographs. ¹The combined figure uses an independence approximation across modeled categories. Hit chance is not the same as profit chance or positive ROI. Entry coverage measures checklist entries connected to at least one published odds row; it does not mean every possible parallel has published odds. Probability-entry coverage and market-value coverage are separate: a player can have complete probability mapping while only a subset of cards has usable market valuations.
