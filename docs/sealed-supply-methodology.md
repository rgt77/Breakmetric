# Sealed Supply evidence methodology

BreakMetric separates **signals** from **measurements**.

A manufacturer product being sold out is useful context, but it does not tell us the percentage of all produced boxes that remain sealed. Likewise, a sealed box selling on a secondary marketplace proves sealed supply still exists, but does not quantify the total.

Public case breaks are treated as a lower bound on opened product. They are not assumed to be representative of all openings because BreakMetric does not know what share of worldwide breaks its sources capture.

## Activation rule

BreakMetric Live Odds remains **scenario-only** until all of the following are true:

1. a sealed-fraction range can be calculated from quantitative evidence;
2. at least two independent quantitative source types support the estimate;
3. serial-number evidence exists for the card being adjusted.

Until then, official manufacturer odds remain the operative probability.

## Current 2026 Topps Chrome Premier League evidence

- Topps US Hobby Box: sold out.
- Topps US sealed Hobby Case: sold out.
- Topps UK Hobby Box: sold out.
- Sealed boxes continue to appear in realized secondary-market sales.
- Public case-break observations prove some product has been opened.
- None of those facts yet supplies a defensible percentage of worldwide sealed product remaining.

This prevents BreakMetric from converting useful market signals into false precision.
