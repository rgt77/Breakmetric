# Implied Hobby production methodology

## Why #201 is useful
Max Dowman base card #201 is Hobby-exclusive. Its numbered parallels therefore avoid a major problem with ordinary parallels: copies cannot be assumed to be allocated entirely to Hobby when a product has multiple formats.

For a Hobby-exclusive numbered parallel:

**implied Hobby packs = print run × published Hobby pack-odds denominator**

BreakMetric calculates the same quantity independently from Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red /5 and Superfractor /1.

## Current result
The five signals form two very tight clusters:

- Gold /50 and Orange /25 imply 1,608,750 Hobby packs.
- Black /10, Red /5 and Superfractor /1 imply 1,544,400 Hobby packs.

BreakMetric therefore stores an implied range instead of selecting one value as certain.

At 20 packs per box and 12 boxes per case, that corresponds to roughly:

- 1,544,400–1,608,750 Hobby packs
- 77,220–80,437.5 Hobby boxes
- 6,435–6,703.125 Hobby cases

These are odds-derived production estimates, not audited manufacturer production records.

## Why the signals disagree
Possible explanations include published-odds approximation, production holdbacks/replacements, packing practices, or another manufacturer-side effect. BreakMetric does not invent a cause.

## Live Odds use
This estimate gives BreakMetric a much stronger denominator for the original Hobby population. It still does **not** tell us what percentage remains sealed today.
