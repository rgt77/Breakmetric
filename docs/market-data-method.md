# BreakMetric market-data methodology

## Primary source
eBay realized/completed sales.

## Rules
1. Use sold/completed sales, never active asking prices.
2. Match the same player, product, insert/autograph set, parallel and serial numbering as closely as possible.
3. Prefer ungraded sales when valuing an ungraded break hit.
4. Store sale date, realized price, currency and source reference.
5. Use the median of comparable sales as BreakMetric's primary market value.
6. Keep low-sale-count cards marked with lower confidence rather than inventing a precise value.
7. When no direct comparable exists, do not silently guess. A separate estimation method must be documented.

## Free workflow
- Standard eBay Completed Listings: recent sold listings.
- eBay Product Research, when available: longer historical window and actual accepted Best Offer values.
- Data is entered/imported into BreakMetric manually in the first version to avoid paid APIs.

## Goal
Keep BreakMetric's infrastructure and market-data acquisition cost at 0 SEK/month during the thesis/MVP phase.
